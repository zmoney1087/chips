# Xiaomi chip identifications from iFixit

- [xiaomi_ifixit_chips.json](xiaomi_ifixit_chips.json): 79 identification entries across 6 device/category records with identifications.
- [xiaomi_ifixit_coverage.json](xiaomi_ifixit_coverage.json): coverage for all 253 device/family records.

Reviewed 253 category pages and 29 teardown, disassembly or Chip ID guides, cross-checked against the 2,114-entry public teardown index. All years are in scope.

Xiaomi-associated English iFixit product categories across all years, teardown/disassembly guides and Chip ID guides. Includes Redmi/POCO and explicitly Xiaomi-labeled ecosystem products; Mibro excluded.

This is a best-effort inventory, not an exhaustive bill of materials. Empty chip arrays mean no identifications extracted in this scan. Some guides are video-only, contain unlabeled photos, or describe disassembly without identifying electronics. Category pages and product-family records do not necessarily correspond to individual models. Source device units may differ by region, storage capacity or revision.

Each identification retains source guide/step links, manufacturer mentions and a tentative flag. Manufacturer mentions are not verified foundry or supplier fields. A false tentative flag means tentative wording was not detected, not independent verification. Entries can describe multiple chips, a stacked package, an RF module, a sensor or a discrete semiconductor. Source errors are possible.

For Xiaomi Mi Band, five explicit source-image IC labels were manually transcribed and linked. Other photos were not systematically OCRed. Videos, reader comments, forum answers, PDF-only labels and the full news archive were not transcribed.
