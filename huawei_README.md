# Huawei chip identifications from iFixit

- [huawei_ifixit_chips.json](huawei_ifixit_chips.json): 246 identification entries across 11 device/category records with identifications.
- [huawei_ifixit_coverage.json](huawei_ifixit_coverage.json): coverage for all 196 device/family records.

Reviewed 197 category pages and 40 teardown, disassembly or Chip ID guides, cross-checked against the 2,114-entry public teardown index. All years are in scope.

Huawei-associated English iFixit product categories across all years, teardown/disassembly guides and Chip ID guides. Includes historical Honor devices nested in Huawei categories; the independent Honor Phone category is excluded.

This is a best-effort inventory, not an exhaustive bill of materials. Empty chip arrays mean no identifications extracted in this scan. Some guides are video-only, contain unlabeled photos, or describe disassembly without identifying electronics. Category pages and product-family records do not necessarily correspond to individual models. Source device units may differ by region, storage capacity or revision.

Each identification retains source guide/step links, manufacturer mentions and a tentative flag. Manufacturer mentions are not verified foundry or supplier fields. A false tentative flag means tentative wording was not detected, not independent verification. Entries can describe multiple chips, a stacked package, an RF module, a sensor or a discrete semiconductor. Source errors are possible.

No systematic OCR of board photos. Only written guide identifications are included unless a source explicitly records another extraction method. Videos, reader comments, forum answers, PDF-only labels and the full news archive were not transcribed.
